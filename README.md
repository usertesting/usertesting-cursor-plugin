# UserTesting for Cursor

**Build faster with AI. Build smarter with human insight.**

When AI makes it easier than ever to build software, knowing *what* to build—and whether it will work for your customers—matters more than ever.

UserTesting brings real customer insights directly into Cursor, helping you make smarter development decisions from the start. Give your AI-powered workflows the human context they need by drawing on existing research, or generate fresh feedback when new questions arise.

Build with greater confidence, reduce guesswork, and create experiences that work for real people—all without leaving Cursor.

## What you can do

### Build with customer insights from the start

Don't start from a blank slate. Access existing customer feedback and research results from your UserTesting workspace to inform what you build, guide development decisions, and give AI more context about what customers actually need.

### Get fresh feedback when you need it

Have a question about a feature, experience, or design? Turn your question into a test plan, configure and launch a UserTesting study using natural language, and gather feedback from real people—without interrupting your development workflow.

### Make better decisions throughout development

Bring customer evidence into your workflow to validate assumptions, identify usability issues, and guide improvements. Whether you're exploring an idea, building a feature, or refining an experience, keep human insight connected to the work.

## Try it in Cursor

Here are a few ways to get started:

**Build with existing insights**

- "Find existing UserTesting research that could help inform this checkout experience."
- "What customer feedback do we have about our onboarding experience?"

**Generate new feedback**

- "Create a usability test to evaluate this new checkout flow."
- "Help me build a test plan to understand whether users can complete this task."

**Improve what you're building**

- "Review the available feedback and suggest improvements to this experience."
- "What usability issues should I prioritize based on our existing research?"

## Setup

1. Add the UserTesting plugin from the Cursor Marketplace.
2. Authenticate with your UserTesting account when prompted.
3. Get started by asking Cursor: "Show me my UserTesting accounts."
